# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good intent while
reviewing evidence, welcome different experience levels and backgrounds, and focus criticism on
ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sexualized
attention, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the repository's private
contact or security-reporting channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens the community. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical; no fixed response time is promised.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.
